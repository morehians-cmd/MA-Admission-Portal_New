If you notice an error in your submitted application, request a correction here. Your original application will remain unchanged while the Admissions Office reviews your request.

Important identity-verification policy. Corrections to your legal name, date of birth, place of birth, and other identity-related details require verification. You may upload supporting documents through this private process or request an in-person verification visit and present a valid government-issued ID or another ID accepted by Moreh Academy. Uploading a file does not automatically approve a correction.
